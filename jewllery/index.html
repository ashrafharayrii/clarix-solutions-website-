Our Process

From Vision to Masterpiece

Four meticulous steps that transform your dream into a heirloom worth passing down.

01
Design Consultation
Sit with our senior designer to discuss your vision, occasion, and preferences — from stone type to metal finish and setting style.
02
Custom Crafting
Our master jewelers hand-craft every component — casting the metal, setting each stone by hand, and applying the finest surface finish.
03

Every completed piece is inspected under 10× magnification and tested for metal purity, prong security, and stone clarity before certification.
04
White-Glove Delivery
Your piece arrives in our signature presentation box, accompanied by GIA documentation, a care guide, and a lifetime craftsmanship warranty.
Our Metals

Crafted in the Finest Gold

We work exclusively with 18 karat gold in three classic hues — each with its own character and warmth.

18K Yellow Gold
The classic choice — a rich, warm tone that has adorned royalty for millennia. 75% pure gold alloyed for lasting durability.
18K White Gold
Platinum-like brilliance at a fraction of the weight. Rhodium-plated for an ultra-bright, mirror-like finish that enhances diamond sparkle.
18K Rose Gold
A romantic blush hue created by a copper alloy blend — timeless, flattering on all skin tones, and utterly distinctive.
