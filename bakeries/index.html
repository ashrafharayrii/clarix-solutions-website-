Why We're Different

Craft You Can Taste

Behind every loaf and pastry is a process refined over years — science, patience, and an obsession with detail.

Live Fermentation
Our sourdough starter has been alive since 2019. Three wild yeast strains cultured from local Amman wheat give our bread a flavour impossible to replicate.
27-Layer Lamination
Our croissants are hand-folded through 27 butter layers. The process takes 72 hours and produces the honeycomb crumb structure that shatters on the first bite.
Amman Exclusives
The Knafeh Danish and Labneh Za'atar flatbread exist nowhere else. We fusion-engineer Levantine flavours into European techniques — a morning ritual unique to this city.
Data-Driven Baking
Every batch is logged — temperature, hydration, fermentation time, yield. Our dashboard tracks it all in real time so quality never slips on a busy Friday morning.
